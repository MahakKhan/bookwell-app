# Bookwell screencast: shot list and narration (target 5–6 min)

Shopify requires a 3–8 minute video that shows the onboarding flow, the merchant flow and the customer flow.
Upload to **YouTube as "Unlisted"** (Vimeo and Google Drive are not accepted) and paste the link into
Partner Dashboard > App listing > "Screencast URL".

## Before you record
- Use a **fresh development store** with the password page OFF, and the app freshly installed (so onboarding shows).
- Record only the browser window, 1920x1080 or 1280x720 (Windows: Win+G Game Bar, or Loom / OBS).
  Use a clean browser profile so no personal tabs, bookmarks or extensions show.
- Do **not** show passwords, API keys or your Partner login. Use made-up customer names/emails.
- Turn on a Shopify **Bogus test gateway** (Settings > Payments > Manage > Test mode) for the deposit step.
- Speak (or add captions). Keep each step slow enough to read.

## Shots

| # | Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|

| 6 | 3:30–4:10 | Back in admin: **Bookings** and **Calendar** show the new booking; open it | "The booking appears right away in Bookings and on the Calendar." |

## Reviewer-friendly tips
- Show the **free-trial approval screen** in Billing (it is a test charge on development stores).
- Mention that privacy webhooks and uninstall cleanup are implemented (not needed on screen).
- Keep the exact steps in "Testing instructions" (already in the listing) consistent with the video.
